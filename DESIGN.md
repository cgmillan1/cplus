# C Plus · Design system

Taken from the C Plus logo: orange and brown buildings over three roofs, and the "we add a plus" idea.

## Colors
| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Night | `#110f0d` | Hero, dark bands, app headers, footer |
| Paper | `#f6f1ea` | Light sections |
| Orange | `#ec9c3b` | Buttons (dark text on top), accents, the "+" |
| Light orange | `#f5b259` | Accents on dark backgrounds |
| Roof brown | `#7c4604` / `#9a5a0e` | Logo, gradients |
| Orange ink | `#8f530c` | Orange text on light backgrounds (5:1) |
| Muted | `#655d53` | Secondary text |

## Type
- **Bricolage Grotesque 800:** headings and the C+PLUS wordmark.
- **Figtree 400 to 700:** body text, 16 to 17px.
- Apps keep Barlow for body text.

## Motion
- Hero and portfolio photos are 3D scenes: photo + depth map, drawn with WebGL (`depth3d.js`).
- "Walk through our work": photos placed in 3D space, scrolling moves the camera.
- Cards tilt toward the pointer; sections rise in on scroll; the process line draws itself.
- Everything turns off with "reduce motion".

## Rules
- Real C Plus photos only. No stock photos, invented reviews or invented prices (the phone mockup is labeled as an example).
- Every screen in Spanish and English.
